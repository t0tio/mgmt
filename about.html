Home About

At InfluencerMGMT LLC, we are dedicated to managing and sourcing top influencers, celebrities, and talent for high-profile activations. Our goal is to turn dreams into successful marketing careers, providing expert guidance and support to help talents thrive.

Founded in 2017 by Chelsea Redmond, we've grown into a leading agency with a faith-forward culture and a combined reach of over 50 million followers.

We're not just about campaigns we're about building careers and creating impact. Our expertise opens doors to unique opportunities for both talents and brands.
